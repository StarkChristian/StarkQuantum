---
term: Fidelity
slug: fidelity
---

A measure of how closely an operation or a state matches what was intended,
usually quoted as a percentage or as an error rate.

Two-qubit gate fidelity is the number that constrains circuit depth, and it
should be read as measured in a full circuit with many gates running at once,
not in isolation. A gate characterised alone will always look better than the
same gate embedded in a busy device.
