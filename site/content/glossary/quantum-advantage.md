---
term: Quantum advantage
slug: quantum-advantage
aka:
  - "Quantum supremacy"
---

A demonstration that a quantum processor performed a task no known classical
method can perform in comparable time.

The claim has two halves: a measured quantum experiment and an estimated
classical baseline. Only the first is measured. There is no proof that the tasks
used are classically hard, so an advantage claim is a statement about the state
of classical algorithms on the day it was made.
