---
term: Physical qubit
slug: physical-qubit
---

A single two-level quantum system that a laboratory can control: a
superconducting circuit, a trapped ion, a neutral atom, a photon, a spin in
silicon. It is unstable by default and requires continuous effort to preserve.

Qubit count in an announcement almost always means physical qubits. On its own
it says how much hardware exists, not what the hardware can do.
