---
term: CRQC
slug: crqc
aka:
  - "Cryptographically relevant quantum computer"
---

A quantum computer large enough and reliable enough to break deployed
public-key cryptography.

Published estimates put this in the range of millions of physical qubits with
sustained error correction. Nothing within several orders of magnitude of that
exists. The term is useful precisely because it names a threshold rather than a
date.
