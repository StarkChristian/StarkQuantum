---
term: CRQC
slug: crqc
aka:
  - "Cryptographically relevant quantum computer"
---

A quantum computer large enough and reliable enough to break deployed
public-key cryptography.

Published estimates for factoring a 2048-bit RSA number range from fewer than a
million noisy physical qubits (Gidney, 2025) to about ten thousand reconfigurable
atomic qubits with longer run times (a 2026 preprint), each under its own
assumptions. All of them assume error correction sustained over a long
computation, which has not been shown at that scale. The term is useful precisely
because it names a threshold rather than a date.
