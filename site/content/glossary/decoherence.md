---
term: Decoherence
slug: decoherence
---

The process by which a quantum system loses its quantum character to its
environment. Stray fields, thermal noise, vibration, and the control wiring
itself all carry information away, after which the superposition is gone and
what remains is classical noise.

Coherence time is how long you have before this dominates. What matters is not
the absolute figure but its ratio to gate time: how many operations fit inside
the window.
