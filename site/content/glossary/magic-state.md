---
term: Magic state
slug: magic-state
---

A specially prepared resource state consumed to perform the non-Clifford
operations that make a quantum computer universal.

Clifford operations alone are classically simulable, so magic states are not
optional. They are produced by distillation, which consumes many noisy copies to
yield one good one, and in most resource estimates distillation dominates the
cost of the entire machine.
