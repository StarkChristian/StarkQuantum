---
term: T gate
slug: t-gate
---

A gate that rotates the phase of a qubit by an eighth of a turn. It is not a
Clifford gate, and adding it to the Clifford gates makes the set universal.

In an error-corrected machine the T gate is usually applied by consuming a magic
state, which makes it the costly operation. The number of T gates in a circuit
also governs how hard the circuit is to simulate classically.
