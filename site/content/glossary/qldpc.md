---
term: qLDPC
slug: qldpc
aka:
  - "Quantum low-density parity-check codes"
---

A family of codes that encode more logical qubits per physical qubit than the
surface code, by giving up the requirement that interacting qubits be
neighbours.

The trade is overhead for connectivity. Whether hardware can supply the
long-range connections these codes need, at the fidelity they need, is one of
the open questions worth watching.
