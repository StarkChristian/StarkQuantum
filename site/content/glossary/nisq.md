---
term: NISQ
slug: nisq
aka:
  - "Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum"
---

The current era of hardware: processors with tens to thousands of physical
qubits, no working error correction, and circuit depths limited by accumulated
noise.

The term was introduced to describe what these machines are, and specifically to
set expectations about what they cannot do. It has since been used in marketing
to suggest a stage on a roadmap. It is a description of a constraint.
