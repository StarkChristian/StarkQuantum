---
term: Shor's algorithm
slug: shor
aka:
  - "Shor’s algorithm"
---

A quantum algorithm that factors integers and computes discrete logarithms in polynomial time. It breaks RSA, Diffie–Hellman and elliptic-curve cryptography on a machine large and reliable enough to run it.

It is the reason larger keys do not help: its cost grows only polynomially with key size.
