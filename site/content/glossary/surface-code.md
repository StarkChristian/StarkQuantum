---
term: Surface code
slug: surface-code
---

The most studied family of quantum error-correcting codes. Qubits sit on a
two-dimensional lattice and only neighbouring qubits interact, which suits
hardware that cannot connect distant qubits.

Its advantages are a relatively high threshold and a layout real chips can
build. Its cost is overhead: roughly a thousand physical qubits per logical
qubit for useful logical error rates, and more under pessimistic assumptions.
