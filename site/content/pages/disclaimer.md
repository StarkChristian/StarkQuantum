## What this site is for

Stark Quantum publishes orientation. The essays exist to help a reader tell a
claim from the evidence behind it, and the briefs exist to record a number and
where it came from.

That is the whole purpose, and it has limits worth stating plainly.

## Not advice

Nothing here is legal advice, investment advice, financial advice, or security
advice, and nothing here is engineering advice for a specific system.

In particular, this site publishes on post-quantum cryptography and on
cryptographic risk. Those pages describe the state of published evidence. They
are not a migration plan, they are not a recommendation about any product, and
they must not be used as the basis for a decision about the security of a real
system. Decisions of that kind need someone who knows your infrastructure,
your data, and your obligations.

No relationship of professional advice arises from reading this site or from
writing to its author.

## Accuracy and currency

Every brief cites a primary source, and the intention is that both the claim
and its limits are stated correctly. Even so, this is one person reading
technical literature, and errors are possible.

Briefs are frozen after publication. If one turns out to be wrong, the
correction appears as a dated note at the top of that brief and the original
text stays where it is. Essays that are marked as living carry a reviewed date
separate from the date of first publication; where those differ, the reviewed
date is the one to trust.

A page that was accurate when written may not describe the field today. Check
the dates, and check the primary source. Corrections are welcome at
<!--email_off-->[info@starkquantum.com](mailto:info@starkquantum.com)<!--/email_off--> and are the most useful
thing you can send.

## External links

Links point to preprints, repositories, and technical pages published by third
parties. Those pages were considered relevant when the link was made, but
their content is not under the control of this site and can change at any
time. No responsibility is accepted for the content of external pages; that
lies with their respective operators.

## Assessment of third-party claims

This site evaluates publicly published technical claims, including claims made
by companies about their own hardware. Those assessments are opinion formed
from the cited sources and are offered as commentary and criticism.

Where a result is described as unconfirmed, narrowed, or not yet demonstrated,
that is a statement about the published evidence available at the time of
writing. It is not an allegation of bad faith on the part of any researcher,
laboratory, or company.
