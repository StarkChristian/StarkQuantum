Claims register

Each factual or numerical claim on the Learn and tool pages, the page that uses it, its source, and what was done with the source. It exists so that a reader can check a number without hunting for it.

What the status means

Checked
The source was read for that claim on {{ checked_on | longdate }}.
Vendor-reported
The figure comes from the company that built the device and has not been independently verified.
Derived here
Computed from data published on this site.
Standard result
Textbook material, cited to its source and not re-derived or re-read on that date.

A claim marked checked shows that the source says what the page says it says. It does not mean the source is right, and a preprint has not been peer reviewed. If you find an error, write to the address in the footer.
