Reality Board

Four questions. Not forty. Each one is a thing that either has happened or has not, and each has a stated condition for changing the answer.

The board exists so that a reader can check the state of the field without reading a year of announcements. Most of these cells will read Not yet for a long time. That is information, not pessimism.

A cell moves only when a human reviews the evidence against the condition written under it. Automated tools may flag a candidate for review. They cannot change a cell.

What is deliberately not on this board

There is no cell for cryptographic threat. The question of when a cryptographically relevant quantum computer arrives is downstream of cells two and four, and giving it its own row would suggest it moves on its own. It does not. It moves when the hardware moves.

There is also no cell for funding, headcount, partnerships, or share price. None of those are evidence about a machine.
