Corrections and changes

Every dated change recorded on a published page, and why. A silent edit is not allowed: a change that is not on this page, or on the page it changed, did not happen.

How changes are recorded

  • Briefs are frozen once published. A correction is a dated note at the top of the brief; the original text stays.
  • Essays and lessons carry a revision history at the bottom of the page.
  • The Reality Board keeps a dated log of every review, including reviews that left the answer unchanged.

Entries that only record first publication are left out. See the methodology for how claims are checked, and write to the address in the footer if you find an error.
